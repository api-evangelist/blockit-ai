# Blockit

> Blockit is an AI scheduling agent. CC it on any email or Slack thread and it reads the thread, proposes times, handles time zones and conflicts, and sends the invite — then reschedules itself when plans change. When both sides use Blockit, the meeting autoblocks in seconds instead of days.

Blockit is made by Blockit AI, Inc. (San Francisco, CA). It is a scheduling
agent, not a booking-link tool: you do not send anyone a link to pick a slot. You CC
`bot@blockit.com` on an email or Slack thread and the agent negotiates the meeting in the
thread, in your voice, then puts it on the calendar.

- One-line pitch: Never touch your calendar again.
- Works with: Google Calendar, Microsoft/Outlook calendar, Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Slack
- Pricing: 30-day free trial, no credit card required
- Security: SOC 2 Type II; encryption at rest and in transit; granular calendar permissions

## Product

- **Crawl — Forward a thread. Get an invite.** The time is already agreed — you just have not written the invite. Forward the thread to Blockit and the invite goes out, titled, described, with the right people and a link attached.
- **Walk — CC it, and keep the last word.** Blockit is in the thread live. It reads the replies, proposes times, handles the time zones — and checks with you before anything lands on a calendar.
- **Run — Let it run while you are gone.** No check-ins. Blockit works through your back-to-backs and overnight, resolving threads in the hours you were never going to answer anyway.

## What it handles

- Reads email and Slack threads and negotiates meeting times end to end

## FAQ

### Can I approve invites before they send?

Yes. Choose your autonomy level — from review-everything to full autopilot — and change it anytime, per person or per meeting type.

### Does Blockit work with Outlook and Teams?

Yes. Google and Microsoft calendars, Zoom, Meet, and Teams links, and Slack. Connect as many calendars as you want; Blockit respects all of them without mixing data between them.

### Can Blockit use our company's domain?

Yes. Teams can run Blockit from their own address — blockit@yourfirm.com — so every external touchpoint carries your brand, not ours.

### Can my EA work with Blockit?

Yes. Blockit doesn't replace great EAs — it takes the scheduling grind off their plate so they can run the higher-order work. Executives keep their own agent; assistants keep visibility.

### Can Blockit handle group scheduling?

Very well. It factors in the free/busy status of colleagues on the thread — and when attendees are also on Blockit, the meeting autoblocks instantly.

### Can I try it free?

30 days, no credit card. CC it on one thread and see.
